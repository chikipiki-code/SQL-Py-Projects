# 🛒 Online Retail Dataset Analysis

**Tech Stack:** MySQL · Python · Jupyter Notebook · Pandas · NumPy · Matplotlib · Seaborn  
**Date:** Jul 2025

---

## 📁 Project Overview
Analyzed a Brazilian e‑commerce dataset (8 CSVs) to answer 15 structured business questions—Basic, Intermediate, and Advanced—to extract KPIs and customer insights for operational improvement.

---

## ✅ Business Questions & Key Findings

### 🔹 Basic Queries
1. **Unique customer cities:** Identified all distinct cities in `customers.csv`.  
2. **2017 order count:** Counted total orders placed in 2017.  
3. **Sales by category:** Found total sales per product category.  
4. **Installment prevalence:** Discovered ~99.99% of orders paid via installments.  
5. **Customers per state:** Tallied customer counts by state.

### 🔸 Intermediate Queries
6. **Monthly orders (2018):** Tracked order volume by month.  
7. **Avg. products per order by city:** São Paulo led with the highest average.  
8. **Revenue share by category:** BED TABLE BATH topped the list.  
9. **Price vs. Purchases correlation:** Negative correlation (cheaper items sell more).  
10. **Seller revenue ranking:** Highlighted top‑performing sellers and performance gaps.

### 🔺 Advanced Queries
11. **Customer moving average:** Computed rolling order‑value averages per customer.  
12. **Cumulative monthly sales:** Visualized month‑by‑month growth for each year.  
13. **YoY sales growth:** Measured year‑over‑year revenue increases.  
14. **6‑month retention rate:** Revealed low repeat‑purchase rates, indicating a need for loyalty programs.  
15. **Top spenders per year:** Identified the top 3 customers by spend in each year.

---

## 📊 Key Insights
- **Top revenue category:** BED TABLE BATH, followed by FURNITURE DECORATION and AUTOMOTIVE.  
- **Installment trend:** Virtually all orders use installment payments—leverage for tailored payment plans.  
- **City focus:** São Paulo shows highest average orders—priority market for targeted marketing.  
- **Seller performance:** Significant revenue disparities—opportunity to standardize seller training.  
- **Retention gap:** Low repeat‑purchase rate—recommend launching loyalty campaigns.

---

## 🛠 Recommendations
- **Loyalty programs** to boost repeat purchases.  
- **Bundled offers** in underperforming categories.  
- **Regional campaigns** focusing on São Paulo.  
- **Seller enablement** based on revenue‑ranking insights.  
- **Payment-plan optimization** aligned with installment usage.  
- **VIP engagement** for top spenders.
